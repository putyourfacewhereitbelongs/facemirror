Trill Face Puppet

A still photo becomes a 2.5D facial shell: 478 landmarks lifted into canonical head space, three hair rings and a pinned frame around them. Your webcam face mesh drives it with a weighted Kabsch fit — pose is separated from expression so a smile stays on the face while the head turns. The unseen far side is approximated from mirrored photo patches rather than generated; the oral cavity uses a rigid jaw, procedural dental arches and a textured tongue. Drag a dot to sculpt, or select Smile, Frown or Mouth O and click the face.

Click-to-sculpt expressions

Pick a tool, then click the face near the mouth (or the brows / cheeks for those tools). The edit is applied in canonical head space, so it rotates with the head. Drag landmarks is the freeform mode: hover a dot to see which feature it belongs to, double-click a dot to reset just that one, Ctrl+Z undoes anything.
