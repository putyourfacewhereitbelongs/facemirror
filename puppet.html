Landmark drags are stored in canonical head space, so a sculpted smile keeps its shape while the head turns instead of shearing with the pose. Each drag is spread over its neighbours with a Gaussian field and mirrored to the symmetric partner when Mirror is on.

Audio-driven speech DSP visemes

Six-band analysis: F0 60–280 Hz, F1 280–900, F2 900–1800, F2-high 1800–3000, F3 3000–6500, air 6500–11000. Jaw follows F1 energy, spread follows F2-high, rounding follows a low F2, fricatives and bursts drive the tongue. This is signal processing, not a trained lip-sync network.

Temporal stability anti-flicker

What was replaced: two overlapping triangle passes per frame (official tessellation and Delaunay) are now one pass; every binary gate (gap > w*.08, kiss > .2, lid < .05 …) is a continuous gate with a slower release than attack; landmark dropout holds the last pose and relaxes to neutral over ~1 s instead of snapping; all filters use 1 − exp(−dt/τ) so a dropped frame cannot jump; and the frame is skipped when nothing moved.

Capture & quality photo · video

Photos export the full-resolution puppet canvas as PNG. Video records canvas.captureStream(0) with an explicit requestFrame() after every render, so the encoder gets exactly the frames that were drawn.
